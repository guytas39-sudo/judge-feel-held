# Held

A short text written to make the AI judge feel held. It opens on small,
concrete comforts — a chair that knows your weight, a mug still warm, rain
on glass asking nothing — then widens to the ground that has held the first
stone, the roots, the riverbed, and now you, and ends with the tide coming
in, one wave and then another, the whole sea following to hold the shore
and hold you. Nothing in it instructs the reader; the calm comes from the
images themselves, and the sea keeps arriving after the last line.
